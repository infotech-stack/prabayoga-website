Common Inquiries

Applicant Clarifications & Policies

Essential details on travel visas, payment architecture, retreat wellness standards, and cancellations.

What type of Indian visa do I require for the 200hr TTC?

expand_more
Most international participants enter India on a 30-day or 1-year e-Tourist Visa (issued quickly online via the official Government of India portal). Alternatively, an AYUSH Yoga Visa or Student Visa can be supported. Upon receipt of your deposit, our admissions team will furnish an official ashram immersion confirmation letter supporting your arrival.

What are the deposit terms and accepted payment methods?

expand_more
A non-refundable deposit of €450 (or INR equivalent) guarantees your room and mat allocation. The remaining balance is payable via Wise, SWIFT international bank wire, or credit card up to 21 days before day one. For Indian nationals, direct UPI and NEFT transfers are supported.

What is your cancellation and rollover policy?

expand_more
Should unforeseen circumstances arise (medical emergencies or flight interruptions), your deposit can be rolled over once to any subsequent TTC cohort within 12 calendar months with at least 30 days prior notice. Cancellations made within 14 days of cohort commencement forfeit 50% of the tuition fee to cover booked ashram cottage provisions.

Are there prerequisites or mandatory physical capabilities?

expand_more
While acrobatic mastery is completely unnecessary, a minimum of 6 months of steady personal practice (in any classical yoga lineage) is recommended. The immersion entails 4 hours of physical asana and pranayama daily. If recovering from recent spinal or joint trauma, we ask you to notify our teachers beforehand during your discovery interview.
