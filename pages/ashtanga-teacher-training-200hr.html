Comprehensive 200-Hour Syllabus

What You Will Learn

A complete foundation in traditional Ashtanga Yoga and the skills required to teach with awareness, knowledge and confidence.

Module 01 self_improvement

Ashtanga Vinyasa Yoga

Complete immersion in the Ashtanga Primary Series (Yoga Chikitsa). Traditional sequencing, Vinyasa and breath-synchronised movement, Surya Namaskara A & B, Standing asanas, Seated asanas, Twists and hip opening, Forward bends, Core strengthening, Arm balances, Backbends, Inversions, Finishing sequence, and Savasana.

Yoga Chikitsa Primary Series • Breath-movement synchronization • Finishing sequence
Module 02 accessibility_new

Asana Alignment & Technique

Foundations and body positioning, joint alignment, spinal alignment, hip and shoulder mechanics, foot and knee alignment. Master safe transitions, identifying and correcting common alignment mistakes, intelligent modifications and variations, use of props, and adapting to individual anatomical differences.

Joint mechanics • Biomechanical precision • Individual modifications & props
Module 03 air

Vinyasa, Breath & Drishti

Ujjayi breathing technique, breath-to-movement coordination, traditional Sanskrit Vinyasa counting, conscious inhalation/exhalation principles, focal gazing points (Drishti), breath awareness in movement transitions, and the internal energetic connection between breath, concentration, and sustained practice.

Ujjayi breath • Drishti focal points • Vinyasa count coordination
Module 04 lock

Bandhas

The three vital internal locks: Mula Bandha (root lock), Uddiyana Bandha (navel lock), and Jalandhara Bandha (throat lock). Explore the direct synergy between Bandhas and breath, the traditional yogic understanding of energetic locks, and safe application during asana transitions and pranayama retentions.

Mula, Uddiyana & Jalandhara Bandhas • Pranic locks during movement
Module 05 groups

Mysore-Style Practice

Structure and lineage of Mysore-style practice. Learn individualised instruction, progressive learning tailored to each student, active observation, diagnosing student holding patterns, providing individualized verbal corrections, safe hands-on adjustments, and supporting unique individual needs.

Self-paced traditional Mysore room • Tailored one-on-one teaching skills
Module 06 wind_power

Pranayama

Traditional classical pranayama: Ujjayi, Nadi Shodhana (alternate nostril breathing), Kapalabhati, Bhastrika, Bhramari (humming bee breath), and cooling breaths (Sitali & Sitkari). Understanding breath awareness, basic breath retention (Kumbhaka), physiological benefits, and crucial precautions & contraindications.

Nadi Shodhana, Kapalabhati, Bhastrika, Bhramari, Sitali & Sitkari
Module 07 psychology

Meditation & Mindfulness

Systematic approaches to inner stillness: Breath meditation (Anapanasati), one-pointed concentration (Dharana), mindfulness meditation, mantra japa meditation, guided meditation techniques, candle gazing (Trataka), an introduction to conscious psychic sleep (Yoga Nidra), cultivating daily awareness and mental steadiness.

Trataka, Mantra meditation, Breath mindfulness, Yoga Nidra basics
Module 08 menu_book

Yoga Philosophy

Patanjali's Yoga Sutras and the Eight Limbs of Yoga (Ashtanga): Yamas & Niyamas, Asana, Pranayama, Pratyahara, Dharana, Dhyana, Samadhi. Study Karma Yoga (selfless action), Svadhyaya (self-study), building awareness, discipline, and inner transformation with selected teachings from the Yoga Sutras and Bhagavad Gita.

Patanjali's 8 Limbs • Bhagavad Gita • Living philosophy & Svadhyaya
Module 09 vital_signs

Anatomy & Physiology

Comprehensive physical study: Skeletal and muscular systems, major muscle groups, spine biomechanics, shoulder and hip anatomy, knee and ankle mechanics. Joint movements including flexion/extension, rotation, and abduction/adduction. Respiratory and nervous systems, safe biomechanics, injury prevention, contraindications, and anatomical modifications.

Biomechanics • Skeletal variations • Injury prevention & nervous system
Module 10 record_voice_over

Sanskrit, Mantra & Chanting

Sanskrit names of asanas and correct vocal pronunciation. Study the Ashtanga opening chant (Vande Gurunam Charanavara Vinde) and Mangala closing mantra (Svasti Prajabhyah). Om chanting, Shanti peace mantras, foundational mantra philosophy, and traditional Sanskrit teaching terminology used in Mysore shalas.

Asana Sanskrit names • Opening & Closing Ashtanga chants • Pronunciation
Module 11 back_hand

Hands-On Adjustments & Assists

The purpose and ethics of adjustments. Verbal and visual corrections versus tactile touch, optimal teacher positioning, applying appropriate and sensitive pressure, respecting individual limitations, student consent and clear communication, knowing when to adjust and when not to adjust, modifying adjustments for injuries, and absolute safety awareness.

Tactile adjustments • Verbal cues • Safety & consent protocols
Module 12 school

Teaching Methodology

Preparing an inspiring yoga class, demonstrating asanas with clarity, delivering precise verbal instructions in both Sanskrit and English terminology. Sharpening student observation skills, diagnosing alignment errors, cueing corrections, modifying for mixed-level rooms, effective voice usage and projection, and cultivating a safe, supportive shala environment.

Voice projection • Demonstration skills • Classroom management
Module 13 conversion_path

Class Sequencing

Master the complete progression flow of classical Ashtanga:

Opening & Centering → Breath Awareness → Warm-up → Surya Namaskara → Standing → Seated → Hip Openers → Twists → Core → Backbends → Inversions → Finishing → Pranayama → Meditation → Savasana

★ Highlight: Includes intelligent adaptation for structured 60-minute classes.

Primary series progression • 60-minute class adaptation
Module 14 diversity_3

Teaching Beginners & Different Levels

Guiding complete beginners safely, coaching regular practitioners, addressing varied flexibility and body types, accommodating students with limited mobility or tight joints, applying intelligent modifications, and safely progressing dedicated practitioners toward intermediate and advanced postures.

Teaching beginners • Modifying for stiffness • Progressive stages
Module 15 verified

Teaching Practicum

The pivotal practical milestone: Teach individual asanas, guide Surya Namaskara A & B, lead designated sections of the Ashtanga sequence, practice live verbal cueing, physical demonstrations, corrections, and hands-on adjustments. Guide pranayama and meditation sessions, culminating in complete class teaching under guided mentorship with detailed constructive feedback.

Hands-on teaching practicum • Live student feedback • Full class delivery
Module 16 balance

Yoga Ethics & Professional Practice

Nurturing sacred teacher-student relationships, healthy professional boundaries, respect and inclusivity, consent frameworks, appropriate physical contact, maintaining confidentiality, teaching within your scope of practice, creating a welcoming and safe learning environment, and upholding noble professional conduct.

Teacher ethics • Consent & touch boundaries • Professional integrity
