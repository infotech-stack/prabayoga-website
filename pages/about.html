Founder & Master Acharya

Yogacharya Prabakaran

Raised in the cultural spiritual cradle of Tamil Nadu, Prabakaran dedicated his early youth to authentic classical yoga under traditional Mysore and South Indian masters. With over sixteen years of relentless personal sadhana, he carries the timeless heritage of traditional Ashtanga Vinyasa into the modern awakening space of Auroville.

His pedagogical foundation synthesizes surgical structural precision, biomechanical safety, breath-initiated movement (Vinyasa Krama), and the profound philosophical tenets of Patanjali’s Yoga Sutras. Rather than imposing rigidity, his keen eye tailors each adjustment to the individual student’s anatomical architecture and energetic field.
