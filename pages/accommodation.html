Practical Information

Accommodation & Living FAQs

Everything you need to know about your residential stay in Auroville.

Check-in begins at 01:00 PM on the day preceding your program commencement to allow you to settle in, explore the gardens, and join the opening orientation circle and welcome dinner at 06:30 PM. Check-out is scheduled at 11:00 AM on the day following graduation. Early check-in or extension nights can be arranged with our admissions coordinator subject to cottage availability.

We offer twice-weekly ashram eco-laundry for all residential students. Clothes are washed using natural Ayurvedic soapnuts and air-dried under the South Indian tropical sun. In addition, each bathroom features a dedicated wash basin with eco-soap flakes for daily handwashing of yoga activewear.

Auroville and Pondicherry experience a pleasant tropical coastal climate. In honoring yogic tradition and bio-climatic architecture, our cottages are constructed with compressed earth, high vaulted roofs, terracotta tile flooring, and perimeter vegetation that naturally keep the rooms significantly cooler than ambient exterior temperatures. All cottages are equipped with silent BLDC ceiling fans. Deluxe cottages feature specialized cross-draft ventilation louvers and mosquito mesh.

Yes. The ashram operates on an uninterrupted solar-backed hybrid microgrid. In the event of local grid power drops, our solar inverter battery system automatically maintains continuous power for lighting, fans, device charging sockets, and high-speed fiber internet routers throughout the campus.

Yes. If you are applying together with a partner, spouse, or friend, you may specify your co-habitant during the application process. For couples, the Deluxe Garden Cottage can be configured with a King bed; for friends, the Shared Twin Sanctum provides two separate comfortable beds.
