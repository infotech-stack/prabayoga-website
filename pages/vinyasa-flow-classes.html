THE TRIAD OF SADHANA

A Practice Designed Around Flow

Grounded in classical traditions, Vinyasa at Praba Yoga is approached not merely as exercise, but as a living meditation.

01

BREATH

Conscious, rhythmic inhalation and exhalation guiding every transition. Breath becomes the steady metronome that calms the nervous system and powers each movement.

02

MOVEMENT

Fluid, intentional asana sequences cultivating strength, mobility, and ease. Moving with architectural precision to safeguard physical joints and expand spatial awareness.

03

AWARENESS

Cultivating steady internal focus and presence throughout the flow. Developing the observant mind (sakshi bhava) to remain serene through physical challenge and stillness alike.
