Bioregional Harmony

An Experimental Earth Dedicated to Human Unity

Founded in 1968, Auroville represents an extraordinary international township experiment in sustainability, collective consciousness, and living in communion with the land.

Our ashram shala rests directly beside the renowned Solitude Farm—a legendary permaculture community where agroforestry, indigenous millet farming, and organic cuisine mirror our yogic discipline. Here, students awaken to the gentle hum of indigenous birdlife, cycle under canopies of neem and gulmohar, and step directly onto warm earthen trails that invite contemplative walking meditation.

Practical Information

Visiting Auroville: Practicalities

What climate should I pack for during TTC courses? expand_more
South India enjoys tropical warmth year-round. Between November and February, temperatures are idyllic and balmy (22°C - 30°C) with pleasant breezes. From March to July, days are warm and dry—ideal for sweating out toxins. We advise loose, breathable natural organic cotton clothing, a sun hat, sandals, and a light wrap for morning meditation.
How does internet connectivity and mobile SIM work? expand_more
Our ashram provides reliable high-speed fiber-optic Wi-Fi throughout living quarters and communal verandas. Indian SIM cards (Airtel or Jio with e-SIM) provide strong 5G reception across the entire Auroville bioregion and can easily be activated upon arrival at Chennai airport.
Do I need a special visa to stay and train at PRABA YOGA? expand_more
Most international students arrive smoothly on a 30-day, 1-year, or 5-year Indian e-Tourist or e-Conference Visa. Our admissions team sends an official invitation confirmation document once your 200hr TTC seat is reserved to assist your travel documentation.
Is the sanctuary safe for solo women travelers? expand_more
Auroville is renowned worldwide as one of the safest and most welcoming environments for international solo female seekers. The international community, gated retreat grounds, dedicated night escorts, and caring ashram staff ensure complete peace of mind.
