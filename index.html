Comprehensive Syllabus

200-Hour Ashtanga Yoga Teacher Training

Deepen Your Practice. Understand the Tradition. Learn to Teach.

Designed for students seeking authentic traditional Ashtanga Vinyasa yoga in a serene immersion setting. Our syllabus adheres strictly to Yoga Alliance standards while honoring timeless Vedic lineage.

01

Ashtanga Vinyasa Yoga

In-depth study and physical mastery of the classical Primary Series (Yoga Chikitsa).

02

Asana Alignment & Technique

Precise geometric breakdowns, therapeutic adaptations, and prop usage.

03

Vinyasa, Breath & Drishti

The Tristhana method: simultaneous harmonization of breath, posture, and gaze points.

04

Bandhas & Energy Locks

Internal subtle energy containment through Mula, Uddiyana, and Jalandhara Bandha.

05

Mysore-Style Practice

Self-directed cadence fostering internal sensory withdrawal (Pratyahara) and focus.

06

Pranayama & Breath Control

Classical Kumbhaka techniques, Nadi Shodhana, Kapalabhati, and Ujjayi breath refinement.

07

Meditation & Mindfulness

Seated dhyana, stillness cultivation, and mindful integration for nervous system equilibrium.

08

Yoga Philosophy & Sutras

Deep study of Patanjali's Yoga Sutras, the Eight Limbs (Ashtanga), and ethical precepts.

09

Anatomy & Biomechanics

Functional musculoskeletal anatomy, joint stabilization, and injury prevention frameworks.

10

Sanskrit, Mantra & Chanting

Opening and closing invocations, correct Sanskrit pronunciation of asana names.

11

Hands-on Adjustments & Safety

Intuitive, safe physical tactile cueing guided by experienced Mysore adjustments.

12

Teaching Methodology

Voice projection, sequencing, student feedback, ethics, and live teaching practicum.

Frequently Asked Questions

The 200-Hour Ashtanga Yoga Teacher Training runs for 21 days. Batches run every month starting on the 3rd and concluding on the 23rd. Students typically arrive on the 2nd to settle into accommodation and depart on the 24th morning.
Yes. Praba Yoga is a certified RYS 200 (Registered Yoga School) with Yoga Alliance USA. Upon successful completion of practical assessments and written exams, you receive an internationally recognized 200-Hour certificate enabling you to register as an RYT 200.
Both Sharing and Standard Room packages include 21 nights of accommodation on-site or adjacent to the Shala, full tuition, the Praba Yoga printed study manual, and daily freshly prepared vegetarian breakfast (seasonal fruits, South Indian grains, herbal infusions). Lunch and dinner can be enjoyed at nearby Auroville organic cafes or ordered locally.
The registration deposit (€300) can be settled online in EUR, GBP, or USD via PayPal or international bank transfer. The remaining balance can be paid on arrival via cash (EUR/USD/INR) or card.
Most international students fly into Chennai International Airport (MAA), which is approximately a 2.5 to 3 hour scenic drive along the East Coast Road. We coordinate trusted pre-arranged private taxi pickups that wait for your flight at arrivals and bring you directly to Praba Yoga.
